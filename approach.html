Founder Expertise: Leadership in Finance and Technology

Paradigm Shift was founded by Alex and Max, two industry veterans with a combined 40 years of experience in finance, technology, and sustainable investing. Alex has led multi-billion dollar portfolios with a focus on strategic asset allocation, risk management, and sustainable investments. Max, a tech innovator with deep expertise in AI and data analytics, co-founded a successful fintech startup and has driven technological advancements in the financial sector. Together, they bring a unique blend of skills that define Paradigm Shift’s distinct approach.

Applying ESG Criteria: Investing with Impact and Responsibility

We integrate ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) criteria into every investment decision, ensuring that our portfolios not only seek financial returns but also contribute positively to the world.

  1. Rigorous Screening: Potential investments undergo stringent ESG screening to exclude companies with poor environmental practices or weak governance.
  2. Sustainability Scoring and Monitoring: Investments are scored based on ESG performance, with ongoing monitoring to maintain alignment with sustainable standards.
  3. Transparent Reporting: We provide clear reporting on the ESG impact of investments, offering clients insight into how their portfolios contribute to positive change.

Technology Validation: Proven, Reliable, and Continually Evolving

Our AI and machine learning models are validated through rigorous testing to ensure they deliver accurate, reliable results.

  1. Backtesting and Simulated Performance: AI models are thoroughly tested against historical data to evaluate predictive accuracy.
  2. Beta Testing and Early Results: Our six-month beta testing phase showed a simulated annual return of 12.8%, outperforming benchmarks by 4.2%. This phase allowed us to refine our models and demonstrate real-world resilience.
  3. Continuous Refinement: Feedback loops between AI systems and human advisors ensure models remain adaptive and relevant to current market conditions.

Comprehensive Risk Disclosure: Managing Uncertainty with Confidence

We recognize that investing involves risk, and we are committed to managing these risks proactively.

  1. Advanced Risk Assessment: Our risk management tools assess portfolio exposure to market volatility, economic changes, and specific operational risks unique to new businesses.
  2. Scenario Planning and Stress Testing: We use these methods to evaluate potential impacts on portfolios, ensuring they are prepared for various market conditions.
  3. Protective Strategies: Techniques such as tax-loss harvesting and asset location are employed to mitigate downside risks and optimize returns.
