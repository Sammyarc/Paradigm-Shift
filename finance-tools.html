Our Financial Tools

MSG ESG Research:

The MSG ESG research API serves as a comprehensive foundation for AI-driven sustainable investment analysis by providing granular environmental, social, and governance data across global markets. This API delivers real-time access to over 10,000 companies' ESG performance metrics, including carbon emissions data, board diversity statistics, and corporate governance scores.


Our AI system leverages this data stream to identify emerging ESG trends, assess companies' sustainability commitments, and evaluate their implementation progress. For instance, when analyzing a potential investment, the AI can instantly compare a company's environmental performance against industry peers, flag any concerning ESG controversies, and track year-over-year improvements in sustainability metrics.


The API's standardized scoring methodology enables our AI to perform consistent cross-company comparisons while its historical database allows for pattern recognition in ESG performance over time, helping predict future sustainability trajectories.



Sustainalytics by Morningstar:

Sustainalytics by Morningstar's API integration enables our AI advisor to conduct in-depth ESG risk assessments and provide clients with forward-looking sustainability insights. The API delivers detailed ESG risk ratings, controversy assessments, and industry-specific materiality indicators for over 12,000 companies globally.


Our AI system processes this data to identify unmanaged ESG risks that could impact financial performance, evaluate companies' risk management capabilities, and assess their exposure to industry-specific ESG challenges.


The API's controversy tracking feature allows our AI to monitor ongoing ESG incidents in real-time, automatically adjusting investment recommendations based on severity and company response. Additionally, the platform's product involvement screening data helps the AI align investment recommendations with clients' specific values and exclusion criteria, while its corporate governance research supports analysis of management quality and shareholder rights issues.



Climate Bond Initiative:

The Climate Bond Initiative API provides our AI advisor with specialized data on green bonds and climate-aligned investments, essential for constructing environmentally conscious portfolios. This API delivers comprehensive information on certified green bonds, including detailed project categories, impact metrics, and alignment with international climate targets.


Our AI utilizes this data to identify genuine climate-positive investments, assess their environmental impact, and evaluate their contribution to climate change mitigation and adaptation goals. The API's taxonomy-alignment checking capabilities enable the AI to verify whether investments truly meet recognized green standards, helping prevent greenwashing in portfolio recommendations.


Real-time tracking of new green bond issuances allows our AI to identify emerging sustainable investment opportunities, while aggregated market data helps it analyze trends in sustainable finance and predict future market developments. The API's impact reporting metrics also enable the AI to quantify and report the environmental benefits of clients' sustainable investments, such as carbon emissions avoided or renewable energy capacity added.
